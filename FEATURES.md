## Planned Capabilities

Development will proceed incrementally, starting with a useful standalone application before introducing distributed coordination.

### Phase 1: Farm Management

Establish the foundation for individual farm nodes.

Potential capabilities include:

- Agricultural records and farm operations management.
- Crop and production tracking.
- Environmental sensor data collection.
- Image-based assistance with identifying pests and plant diseases.
- Local data storage and backup.
- Web-based interfaces suitable for desktop and tablet devices.

### Phase 2: Farm-to-Farm Collaboration

Enable communication between participating farms.

Potential capabilities include:

- Sharing observations about pests, diseases, and weather conditions.
- Exchanging production information and agricultural knowledge.
- Coordinating equipment and machinery sharing.
- Discovering available resources across the network.
- Defining permissions and policies for shared information.
- Synchronizing selected data between nodes.

### Phase 3: Cooperative Coordination

Introduce capabilities that support collective organization.

Potential capabilities include:

- Cooperative production planning.
- Shared resource scheduling.
- Coordination of supply and distribution.
- Collective product branding and commercialization.
- Aggregated production insights.
- Cooperative governance and decision support.

These phases describe the intended direction of the project, not a fixed development roadmap.
