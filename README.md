# Rizoma

**Independent farms. Stronger together.**

Rizoma is an open-source platform for agricultural cooperatives, designed to help small farms manage their operations, share knowledge and resources, and collaborate through a decentralized network.

## Vision

Small farms shouldn't have to operate in isolation to remain independent.

By working together, farmers can share equipment, exchange knowledge, coordinate production, respond to common challenges, and build stronger collective identities in the market.

Rizoma aims to provide the digital infrastructure for that cooperation without requiring farms to surrender control over their operations or data.

The guiding principle is simple:

**Each farm operates independently. Together, they achieve more.**

## How It Works

Rizoma is designed around a network of independent nodes.

Each participating farm runs its own instance of the application, responsible for managing local operations and storing its data.

Nodes will eventually be able to communicate with one another, exchanging information and coordinating activities according to agreements between participating farms.

A cooperative coordination layer may provide shared services and facilitate collective decisions without becoming a mandatory dependency for local operations.

### Core Principles

- **Local autonomy:** Each farm maintains control over its operations and information.
- **Offline-first:** Essential functionality should remain available without an internet connection.
- **Selective sharing:** Farms decide what information and resources they make available to others.
- **Cooperation over centralization:** Collaboration should strengthen individual farms rather than make them dependent on a central operator.
- **Accessible infrastructure:** The software should run on affordable hardware, including small computers such as Raspberry Pi devices.
- **Open source:** The platform should be transparent, extensible, and available for communities to adapt to their needs.

## Architecture

Rizoma follows a **local-first, distributed architecture**.

Each farm node is intended to operate as a self-contained application with its own local data storage and business logic.

Network communication and cooperative coordination will be introduced progressively.

## Project Status

**Early planning and development.**

Rizoma is currently an experimental project. Its architecture, features, and development priorities are subject to change as the design evolves.

The initial focus is on building a reliable, useful standalone farm node before addressing the complexities of distributed systems.
